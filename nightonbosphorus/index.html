Before you board.

How much does the cruise cost?

Tickets are €25 for the alcohol-free package and €40 for the package with alcohol. Both include dinner, the live show and round-trip hotel transfer.

How do I pay?

Pay on board by cash or card.

Is hotel transfer included?

Round-trip hotel transfer is included. Confirm that your hotel is covered when requesting a table.

Can children come on the cruise?

Yes. Ask about age-based prices when requesting a table.
